# StartEngine

> StartEngine is a Los Angeles based online private-market investment platform. Investors
> buy equity in startups and pre-IPO companies; founders raise capital under Regulation
> Crowdfunding, Regulation A+ and Regulation D. StartEngine operates a FINRA-registered
> funding portal (StartEngine Capital), a later-stage pre-IPO vehicle (StartEngine Private),
> a secondary marketplace for eligible private shares, and StartEngine Secure, an
> SEC-registered transfer agent and cap-table management product sold to issuers.

## Status of the API surface

StartEngine publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-05 there is no developer portal, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no documented webhooks, and no MCP or A2A surface.
`api.startengine.com`, `developer.startengine.com` and `docs.startengine.com` do not resolve.
`www.startengine.com/robots.txt` disallows `/api` for all crawlers, indicating the only `/api`
path is the site's own internal backend. Every `/.well-known/*` probe is answered by an HTML
catch-all (HTTP 202), confirmed against a control path — do not read those responses as hits.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from the public record; it is not published by
StartEngine.
